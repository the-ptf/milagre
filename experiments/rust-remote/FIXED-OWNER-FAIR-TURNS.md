## Queue and turn contract

Each slot retains a checked reuse generation and at most one canonical device, exact owner ID and fixed outcome. Admission searches from the last visited slot, immediately behind the next cursor; the initial cursor is zero with slot fifteen as that initial search origin. Occupied slots never move. Full returns the exact original unqueued attempt. A successful insert validates vacancy and the next generation before changing the bucket, row or consuming the attempt. Reuse exhaustion records BucketOverflow and sticky Abort, returns Stopped and installs no entry. The existing permit Drop then requests cancellation without opening Begin.

The cursor visits one physical slot per fixed-mode round and advances even when that slot is empty, stale, cancelled or blocked by an occupied same-device core. A queued bucket is released only on exact-ticket selection or terminal cleanup. A stale ticket cannot take a reused slot, even if supplied with its successor's current owner ID. The two retained core identities remain separate from queued slots; a selected or draining claim does not occupy a queue bucket. Existing core disposal and no-replay evidence remain unchanged.

A round first checks stop and runs existing cancellation/deadline/delivery maintenance. It handles at most one reserved control command, then at most one ordinary command, then one private preparation and one bucket visit. The ordinary envelope is extracted only after control execution resolves. Maintenance and stop are checked before each command; existing shared final commits still check exact identity, fresh time, cancellation and overflow. An unwind leaves the unextracted ordinary envelope in the owned queue for normal rejection and drainage. The eight-slot control reserve and out-of-band stop remain available.

A continuously eligible bucket receives a visit within sixteen physical visits despite another device's repeated submissions or traffic in both command lanes. Starting additionally requires a free core at that visit. This promises neither preemption nor a wall-clock bound. The unchanged bounded all-bucket runnable scan permits parking with two occupied cores, same-device blockage, peerless Ready or delayed return. The actor still uses checked generation/unpark and the bounded 1..100 ms maintenance tick. Existing maintenance/emit/consume may each poll the two retained entries; this is not a new one-poll-per-round claim.

Registry remains the timeout authority. Maintenance does not publish Expired from a newer independent row-clock sample. Duplicate preparations can remain visible while another attempt owns the same token's extended Registry claim; their same-device bucket remains blocked, then receives exact stale/terminal cleanup without execution or false promotion.
